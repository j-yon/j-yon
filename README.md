### About Me
- 🧪 Ph.D. student in the [Sherrill Group](https://vergil.chemistry.gatech.edu/) at Georgia Tech, working with Psi4 for accurate *ab initio* methods for molecular crystals
- 🎓 NDSEG Fellow · CoS Dean's Doctoral Fellow
- 🦃 B.S. Chemistry & B.S. Computer Science from Virginia Tech
- 🚀 Research intern at NASA Glenn Research Center

### Current Interests
- **Local coupled-cluster methods for molecular crystals:** testing how well DLPNO approximations reproduce crystal lattice energies under the many-body expansion
- **Symmetry-aware cluster generation:** software that automates many-body expansion computations by exploiting crystal symmetry
- **High-performance computing for quantum chemistry:** developing GPU-accelerated implementations of *ab initio* methods
